# Useful Life

*Land is a non-depreciable asset. The estimated useful lives of our buildings are up to thirty years. Leasehold improvements and finance leases are amortized based on the lesser of either the asset’s estimated useful life or the expected remaining lease term. Construction in process represents assets that are not available for their intended use as of the balance sheet date.*
